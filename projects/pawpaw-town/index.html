IN CONTEXT

A playable cat-game prototype

Pawpaw Town is a small isometric game where you control a cat exploring rooms in a house. In Unity I worked on movement, jumping, and object interactions so the environment reacts to what the cat does.

The workshop, kitchen, and attic have different objects and movement challenges. This began as a game idea I considered publishing on Steam, but the footage here is from a prototype, not a released game.
