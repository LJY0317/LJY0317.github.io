PhotoArchiveKit

Review duplicate photos before cleaning up.

View code ↗

PhotoArchiveKit scans local folders for exact duplicate files. You can compare matches and decide what to remove. It also handles Live Photos.

  • Scan multiple folders for exact duplicates.
  • Compare results in a SwiftUI app.
  • Move selected files to Trash or quarantine, and restore them.
