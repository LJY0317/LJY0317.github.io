Context

What the team built

The team platform covered ETF information alongside deposits and savings products, external video and news content, recommendation features, user profiles, and community functions.

Scope boundaryThe repository assigns a defined subset of numbered features to me. This page describes that subset and does not claim ownership of the entire product or its complete interface design.

Responsibility

From raw market data to usable views

Problem

ETF browsing depends on consistent data collection and update behaviour. Duplicate records, expensive inserts, incomplete histories, or mismatched API responses quickly surface as incorrect filters, charts, and detail pages.

Action

  • Collected more than 1,000 ETF records and daily market data through PyKRX-backed workflows.
  • Used Django management commands, bulk insertion, uniqueness constraints, duplicate prevention, and incremental updates.
  • Implemented ETF search, filters, sorting, pagination, detail information, and historical chart views.
  • Integrated external content flows including YouTube and Naver News APIs.
  • Performed bug testing, fixes, performance work, and code refactoring within the assigned feature set.

Result

The project produced repeatable data-loading workflows and user-facing ETF exploration features built on the same structured records. The repository documents the implementation, but no curated public screenshots or videos were supplied for this portfolio update.
