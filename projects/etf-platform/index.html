Context

What the team built

A two-person project covering ETFs, savings products, news and video content, recommendations, and community features.

Responsibility

From ETF data to charts

Problem

ETF data changes daily. Preventing duplicates and keeping historical records consistent matters for accurate search results and charts.

Action

  • Collected more than 1,000 ETF records and daily market data through PyKRX-based workflows.
  • Used Django management commands, bulk inserts, uniqueness constraints, duplicate checks, and incremental updates.
  • Implemented ETF search, filtering, sorting, pagination, detail views, and historical charts.
  • Integrated external content from the YouTube and Naver News APIs.
  • Tested and fixed bugs, improved performance, and refactored the assigned features.

Result

I added bulk inserts, duplicate checks, and incremental updates so the data can be refreshed repeatedly.
