Context

What the team built

The team platform combined ETF information with deposit and savings products, external video and news content, recommendations, user profiles, and community features.

Scope boundaryThe repository identifies the features I was responsible for. This page covers that scope without attributing the entire product or interface to me.

Responsibility

From market data to usable views

Problem

Reliable ETF browsing depends on consistent collection and updates. Duplicate records, inefficient inserts, incomplete histories, or mismatched API responses can surface as incorrect filters, charts, and detail pages.

Action

  • Collected more than 1,000 ETF records and daily market data through PyKRX-based workflows.
  • Used Django management commands, bulk inserts, uniqueness constraints, duplicate checks, and incremental updates.
  • Implemented ETF search, filtering, sorting, pagination, detail views, and historical charts.
  • Integrated external content from the YouTube and Naver News APIs.
  • Tested and fixed bugs, improved performance, and refactored the assigned features.

Result

The result was a repeatable data-loading workflow and ETF exploration features backed by the same structured records. The implementation is documented in the repository, but no reviewed screenshots or videos are available for publication.
