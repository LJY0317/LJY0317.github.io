Robotics and system integration · Six-person team · Feb–Mar 2026

Autonomous Greenhouse Robot & Navigation System

A greenhouse robot simulation combining mapping, navigation, route behaviour, and an operator-facing control interface.

My role

ROS 2 simulation and navigation work, with frontend integration for robot controls.

Selected work

  • Integrated camera, LiDAR, and IMU sensors with differential drive, SLAM configuration, and map save/reload.
